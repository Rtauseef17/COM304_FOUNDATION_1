**NOTE**

* At the conclusion of the module, describe how you have progressed.

* At the conclusion of this module, I can clearly recognise how significantly my technical understanding, practical capability, and confidence have developed. At the start, my knowledge of embedded systems, cloud computing, and operating system structures was limited to theoretical ideas. Through the hands-on sessions, I gained practical experience configuring Raspberry Pis, working directly with Linux, and understanding how hardware and software interact at a fundamental level. This allowed me not only to develop competence in system administration and programming but also to appreciate how these technologies function in real organisational contexts.

* My progression is most evident in my ability to apply technical concepts to real-world proposals. For example, learning about GPIO interactions enabled me to design feasible IoT solutions, while the cloud computing exercises provided the foundation to propose scalable, modern infrastructure for council operations.

* Another key area of growth has been my critical thinking and analytical skills. Completing SWOT and PEST analyses for each proposal helped me think strategically and understand how technology decisions affect organisations. This broader perspective is something I did not have at the beginning of the module, and it has strengthened my ability to justify decisions professionally and academically.

* Overall, this module has significantly enhanced both my technical skills and my ability to think like an IT professional. I have progressed from having a foundational interest in computing to being able to design, evaluate, and propose technology solutions grounded in practical experience. This development has prepared me for more advanced study and for contributing meaningfully to real world digital transformation initiatives.


* What have you learnt that is new.

* the session on assembly-level programming using CPUlator was unlike anything I had done before. Understanding how a CPU executes instructions at the register level helped me appreciate how high-level code is ultimately processed by hardware

* Another major area of new learning was the Linux operating system. Although I had some familiarity with computers, I had never worked extensively in a command line environment. During the sessions, I learned how Linux handles files, permissions, processes, and software packages. Understanding the structure of an operating system, as well as how software is installed, configured, and managed, gave me a much clearer view of how computers operate behind the scenes.

* What have you struggled with.

* I struggled with CPUlator i found it difficult to understand at first but with testing it multiple times in the classroom i now have a basic understanding how it works.

* Another thing i struggled with was the Logic gates

* What personal research have you done.

* I read multiple websites during my own time to learn more about transistors, CPUlator, Caches and more. 

* What will you need to do next to consolidate your knowledge.

* To consolidate the knowledge I have gained throughout this module, I recognise that I need to continue practising and building upon the technical and analytical skills I have developed. One of my priorities will be to reinforce my understanding of Linux system administration. Although I have gained confidence in using the command line and managing software packages, I need more hands on experience to make these skills second nature. Regularly working with Linux, exploring different distributions, and experimenting with system configuration will help strengthen this foundation.

I also want to continue developing my competence with the Raspberry Pi and IoT technologies. The sessions introduced me to hardware programming, GPIO control, but there is still a great deal to explore. Building additional small projects such as sensor based automation systems or IoT data dashboards will help me deepen my understanding of how hardware, software, and networking integrate in real world applications.
