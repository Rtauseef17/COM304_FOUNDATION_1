# Council Proposal 1

## Explanation of this proposal
*What do you think this proposal might mean? How might it be implemented?*

Through interactions with Linux, open-source development tools, and community-driven software ecosystems throughout the sessions, I have gained an appreciation for the benefits of open-source software. I therefore propose that the council adopt an “open-source first” procurement strategy where feasible. Open-source solutions reduce licensing costs, enhance transparency, encourage innovation, and prevent vendor lock-in. My direct experience using open-source tools such as Linux and WiringPi has shown me how robust, flexible, and secure open-source solutions can be when properly managed.

General exposure across sessions to open-source tools such as WiringPi, Linux commands, and Raspberry Pi environments.

Open-source success cases in government (UK Gov Digital Service, NHS initiatives)

## Evaluation of the proposal
*Povide a breif evaluation based on what you know*

Throughout the module, especially in Sessions 1 and 7, I worked with open-source tools such as Linux and WiringPi, which demonstrated their reliability and flexibility. Preferring open-source software could significantly reduce licensing costs and lower vendor dependency. However, the council would need adequate in-house skills to maintain and support such systems. Based on my exposure to open-source ecosystems and package management, I believe this proposal is practical and strategically beneficial, provided training and governance frameworks are in place
