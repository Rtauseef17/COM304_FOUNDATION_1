# Council Proposal 1

## Explanation of this proposal
*What do you think this proposal might mean? How might it be implemented?*

Building on my learning, I propose implementing a robust VPN solution that allows counsellors to work securely from home. VPN access would encrypt all communications and provide authenticated entry into council systems, ensuring confidentiality and data integrity. Given my understanding of authentication protocols, encrypted communication channels, and remote administration techniques, I am confident in recommending a secure remote-work infrastructure that balances accessibility with security
